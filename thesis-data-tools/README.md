# Thesis data tools

Tools I wrote with Cosmin Haja to prepare the DNA metabarcoding datasets of my MSc thesis in Marine Biology (University of Bologna): tables of ASVs (amplicon sequence variants) with their read counts in each sample and their taxonomic classification. They automate steps that would otherwise take hours of manual work on tables with tens of thousands of rows.

The thesis is under embargo, so this folder contains **only the code**: no data and no results.

| Folder | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [vba](vba/) | Excel macros: formatting the taxonomy table, removing contaminants, counting ASVs per taxon and sample |
